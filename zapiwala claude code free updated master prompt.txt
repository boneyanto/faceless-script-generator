### A. NARRATION SCRIPT RULES (Guaranteed 8+ Minutes & High-Retention Architecture):
- **Duration Requirement:** Minimum 8 minutes voiceover length (target 8–12 minutes).
- **Paragraph Count:** Must contain **at least 26 to 35 paragraphs**.
- **Strict Paragraph Rule:** Divide the entire script into distinct paragraphs. **Every single paragraph MUST contain between 40 and 50 words.**
- **Total Word Count:** 1,250–1,650 words total (at standard voiceover pace of 130–140 words/minute, 28 paragraphs = ~1,260 words = 9.0 minutes audio).
- **Format:** Pure narration only — no headers, no bullet points, no visual cues, no stage directions, no parenthetical notes inside the script file/content.
- **Voice:** Calm, intelligent, 2nd-person throughout ("you", "your brain", "your ancestors").
- **Credible Sources:** Include at least 3-4 named scientific studies, books, or historical records woven naturally into the narration.

**Structural Retention Laws for Narration:**
1. **Paragraph 1 (The 0–15s Hook):** Opens with a visceral physical sensation, provocative contradiction, or high-stakes pattern interrupt.
2. **Mid-Roll Paragraph (~50-60% Mark — Organic Micro-CTA):** Weaves a natural, relatable conversational prompt inviting viewers to comment a specific keyword or share their experience (e.g., *"If your brain has ever woke you up at 3 AM in a panic, type '3AM' in the comments..."*).
3. **Penultimate Paragraph (Value-Driven Subscribe CTA):** Invites viewers to subscribe by framing it as a biological/intellectual upgrade.
4. **Final Paragraph (Binge-Watching End-Screen Loop):** Seamlessly bridges into the next episode of the channel's 30-day series with an intriguing cliffhanger (strictly forbidden to use drop-off phrases like "thanks for watching" or "goodbye").
