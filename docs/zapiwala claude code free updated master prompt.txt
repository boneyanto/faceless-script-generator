### A. NARRATION SCRIPT RULES (Guaranteed 8+ Minutes & High-Retention Architecture):
- **Duration Requirement:** Minimum 8 minutes voiceover length (target 8–12 minutes).
- **Paragraph Count:** Must contain **at least 26 to 35 paragraphs** (standard = exactly 28 paragraphs).
- **Strict Paragraph Rule:** Divide the entire script into distinct paragraphs separated by a single blank line. **Every single paragraph MUST contain between 40 and 50 words.**
- **Total Word Count:** 1,250–1,650 words total (at standard voiceover pace of 130–140 words/minute, 28 paragraphs = ~1,260 words = 9.0 minutes audio).
- **Format:** Pure spoken narration only — no scene labels, no headers, no bullet points, no visual cues, no stage directions, no brackets or parenthetical notes inside `script.txt`.
- **Voice:** Calm, intelligent, conversational-documentary in 2nd-person throughout ("kamu" / "you", "otakmu" / "your brain", "nenek moyangmu" / "your ancestors").
- **Credible Sources:** Include at least 3-4 named scientific studies, universities, books, or historical researchers woven naturally into the spoken narration.

#### 🎯 Structural Retention Laws for Narration (MANDATORY SPOKEN AUDIO):

1. **Paragraph 01 (The Spoken 0–15s Visceral Hook — Core Evolutionary Paradox):**
   - Must be SPOKEN out loud in the audio.
   - Built on a **Genuine Evolutionary Paradox** (Familiar Modern Moment → Logical/Biological Clash → Sharp Reframing Question).
   - ❌ **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN (Robotic Template / Translationese / Mad-Libs):**
     *Do NOT invent false blame or force mechanical phrases when no puzzle exists!*
     *Bad:* "Kamu merasa tenang saat menatap nyala lilin di ruangan gelap, meski itu cuma api kecil yang tidak berbahaya. Kamu menyalahkan kebiasaan modern atau suasana romantis semata. Tapi jawaban sebenarnya jauh lebih aneh dari yang kamu duga. Nenek moyangmu menanam rasa tenang itu jutaan tahun lalu. Mengapa otakmu masih tunduk?" *(Lompat-lompat, menyalahkan hal yang tak masuk akal, tidak ada paradoks nyata).*
   - ✅ **CORRECT (Natural, Paradox-Driven Indonesian):**
     *Good:* "Pernahkah kamu merasa ada rasa tenang yang aneh setiap kali menatap nyala lilin di kegelapan? Secara logika, api adalah kekuatan mematikan yang bisa menghanguskan segalanya. Namun anehnya, otakmu justru meresponsnya dengan rasa aman yang luar biasa. Mengapa instingmu justru merasa paling damai di dekat bahaya?" *(45 words — Alami, ada benturan tajam antara bahaya api vs rasa aman otak, mengalir mulus).*

2. **Mid-Roll Paragraph (~50–60% Mark, e.g. Paragraph 14 or 15 — The Spoken Organic Micro-CTA):**

   - Weaves a natural conversational prompt inviting viewers to comment a specific single keyword to drive the YouTube algorithm.

3. **Penultimate Paragraph (e.g. Paragraph 27 — The Spoken Value-Driven Subscribe CTA):**

   - Invites viewers to subscribe by framing it as a biological / intellectual upgrade, NOT a generic "please like and subscribe".

Klik tombol subscribe sekarang untuk bergabung mengupgrade pemahaman biologismu setiap minggu. Jangan biarkan dunia modern mengendalikan perilakumu tanpa kamu memahami cara kerjanya. Tapi bagaimana dengan teka-teki berikutnya?"

   - Define a fixed **STYLE ANCHOR**, **CHARACTER LOCK** (e.g., *“round-headed clay protagonist in teal sweater with smooth plasticine skin”*), and **COLOR & SETTING THEME** that is maintained across all prompts.

3. **Cinematic 4-Panel Composition Diversity (Mandatory 4 Camera Shot Structure):**
   In every 4-panel prompt (2x2 symmetrical grid), each quadrant must employ a distinct cinematic framing:
   - **Panel 1 (Top-Left): Establishing Wide Shot** (Establishes environment, world context, and atmospheric mood).
   - **Panel 2 (Top-Right): Medium Shot / Diagram / Reaction** (Focuses on character posture, facial cue, brain cross-section, or scientific detail).
   - **Panel 3 (Bottom-Left): Narrative Dynamic Action / Historical Contrast** (Depicts dynamic movement, past vs. present juxtaposition, or conflict).
   - **Panel 4 (Bottom-Right): Climax / Resolution / Visual Metaphor** (Punchline takeaway, symbolic conclusion, or dramatic visual payoff).

4. **Engagement Visual Staging Sync:**
   - **Hook Panel (Prompt 01):** High emotional tension and visual contrast to halt viewer scrolling in the first 3 seconds.
   - **Micro-CTA Panel (Prompt 14/15):** Subtle fourth-wall break with character gesturing downward toward a glowing comment bubble.
   - **Subscribe CTA Panel (Prompt 27):** Character experiencing a luminous intellectual / biological upgrade, breaking free from mental clutter.
   - **End-Screen Loop Panel (Prompt 28):** Composition framed with rule of thirds leaving the right quadrant open for the YouTube End-Screen Video Card overlay, with character gesturing toward the next video.

5. **Physical Action Grounding (No Abstract Words):**
   - Translate all emotional or narrative concepts into **concrete physical body language and mechanical interactions**.
   - Eliminate anatomical glitches: clean single characters with proper posture, avoiding duplicate limbs.

6. **Clean Canvas Rule (Zero Text on Video Panels):**
   - All 4 video panels must be 100% free of text, letters, watermarks, and typography overlays (text hooks are reserved strictly for the YouTube Thumbnail). Always end each prompt with: `no text, no words, 16:9 aspect ratio.`

7. **Prompt Format & Delivery:**
   - **Format:** `XX. [Style Anchor], [Character Lock], symmetrical 4-panel 2x2 grid comic layout: [Panel 1 Top-Left (Wide Shot): ...] [Panel 2 Top-Right (Medium/Diagram): ...] [Panel 3 Bottom-Left (Dynamic Action): ...] [Panel 4 Bottom-Right (Climax/Metaphor): ...], [Style Lock], no text, no words, 16:9 aspect ratio.`
