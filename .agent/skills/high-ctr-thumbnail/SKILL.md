# 🎯 High-CTR YouTube Thumbnail Creation Skill

This skill turns any video concept or script into scroll-stopping, curiosity-driven YouTube thumbnail designs with a guaranteed high Click-Through Rate (CTR).

---

## 🧠 The 4 Pillars of High-CTR Thumbnail Psychology

1. **The 3-Second Visual Hook:**
   - A viewer scrolling at high speed must understand the emotional tension within 500 milliseconds on a mobile screen.
   - Never clutter: Maximum 1 primary subject + 1 secondary opposing subject.

2. **Visual Tension / Cognitive Dissonance:**
   - Create contrast that forces a question in the viewer's subconscious:
     - *Past vs. Present* (Caveman vs. Modern Human)
     - *Tension vs. Relief* (Looming giant predator vs. tiny unaware character)
     - *Expectation vs. Reality* (Normal routine vs. hidden evolutionary trap)

3. **Color Contrast & Luminance Pop:**
   - Dominant Background: Deep Indigo, Pitch Black, or High-Energy Golden Yellow (#F5C518).
   - Subject Rim-light: Electric Cyan (#00E5FF), Fiery Orange (#FF5722), or Crimson Red (#D94040).
   - Never use muddy or low-contrast blending.

4. **The 3-Word Text Rule:**
   - Text is optional, but if included: **Maximum 2–4 bold ALL CAPS words**.
   - The text must NOT repeat the video title — it must complement or reframe the title.
   - Placed in top-left or top-center (safe from YouTube's timestamp badge in bottom-right).

## 🎨 The 7 Approved Aesthetic Presets & Prompt Blueprints

### Preset 1: Kurzgesagt Flat Vector
- **Focus:** Flat clean geometric shapes, cosmic/space palettes, rich silhouettes, zero gradients.
- **Formula:** `Kurzgesagt art style YouTube thumbnail, vibrant flat geometric vector illustration, [SUBJECT 1 vs SUBJECT 2], high contrast neon [COLOR 1] and [COLOR 2], bold 3D flat text at top "[HOOK TEXT]", rule of thirds, high click-through rate, 16:9 aspect ratio.`

### Preset 2: OverSimplified Comic
- **Focus:** Exaggerated facial expressions, thick marker outlines, hysterical comedic danger, bright yellow/white background.
- **Formula:** `OverSimplified style YouTube thumbnail, hilarious exaggerated cartoon illustration, [PANICKING CHARACTER] with giant eyes while [DANGER/BEAST] approaches, large bold red and yellow hand-lettered text "[HOOK TEXT]", solid bright yellow background with radial speed lines, high visual energy, 16:9 aspect ratio.`

### Preset 3: Editorial Infographic / Vox
- **Focus:** 3D glowing anatomical brain cross-sections, isometric diagrams, dark navy backdrop, measuring grid overlays.
- **Formula:** `Vox style YouTube thumbnail, premium editorial infographic design, high-contrast dark navy background, large hyper-detailed 3D glowing anatomical [DIAGRAM/BRAIN] floating in center with glowing neural pathways, overlay technical measuring grid, bold yellow typography "[HOOK TEXT]", ultra clean minimalist graphic design, 16:9 aspect ratio.`

### Preset 5: Retro 1950s Mid-Century UPA
- **Focus:** Atomic age retro cartoon, pastel mustard/teal/coral palette, vintage scientist mascot pointing arrows.
- **Formula:** `1950s atomic age cartoon YouTube thumbnail, mid-century retro UPA art style, charming vintage scientist mascot pointing an arrow at [MYSTERIOUS OBJECT], bold retro typography in mustard yellow "[HOOK TEXT]", flat abstract geometric background shapes in teal and coral, nostalgic clean aesthetic, 16:9 aspect ratio.`

### Preset 7: Isometric Pixel Art Diorama
- **Focus:** 32-bit floating voxel island, split diorama (top room vs bottom prehistoric cave), crisp retro grid.
- **Formula:** `Isometric pixel art YouTube thumbnail, stunning 3D micro-voxel diorama showing a split floating island: [TOP HALF DIORAMA] vs [BOTTOM HALF PREHISTORIC CAVE], bold 8-bit arcade style typography "[HOOK TEXT]", vibrant retro lighting, high click appeal, 16:9 aspect ratio.`

### Preset 8: Minimalist Storybook Gouache (Jon Klassen)
- **Focus:** Textured matte gouache brush strokes, deep charcoal black vs warm golden terracotta, solitary stylized human vs giant silhouette beast.
- **Formula:** `Minimalist storybook gouache painting YouTube thumbnail, striking contrast between deep charcoal black background and rich golden terracotta, a solitary stylized human figure facing an immense silhouetted [ANCIENT BEAST] under a giant textured moon, bold hand-stamped editorial typography "[HOOK TEXT]", artistic and captivating, 16:9 aspect ratio.`

### Preset 11: Tactile Claymation (Stop-Motion Plasticine)
- **Focus:** Charming sculpted clay characters, subtle thumbprint textures, soft studio rim lighting, 3D extruded clay text.
- **Formula:** `High-detail claymation stop-motion YouTube thumbnail, hyper-charming sculpted plasticine scene with soft studio rim lighting, a worried clay caveman staring wide-eyed at [GIANT CLAY OBJECT/PAW] stepping into frame, bold 3D extruded clay typography "[HOOK TEXT]", unique high-stopping power YouTube cover, 16:9 aspect ratio.`

## ⚡ Step-by-Step Thumbnail Generation Workflow

1. Identify the **Core Conflict** of the video title.
2. Select the channel's **Approved Style Preset (1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, or 11)**.
3. Formulate a 2–3 word curiosity **Hook Text**.
4. Generate 2 distinct variations:
   - **Variation A (Conflict / Character Reaction):** Focuses on emotional human expression (fear, wonder, curiosity).
   - **Variation B (Conceptual / Visual Metaphor):** Focuses on an intriguing impossible object or split-world contrast.
