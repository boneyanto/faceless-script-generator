# 🎯 High-CTR YouTube Thumbnail Creation Skill

This skill turns any video concept or script into scroll-stopping, curiosity-driven YouTube thumbnail designs with a guaranteed high Click-Through Rate (CTR).

---

## 🧠 The 4 Pillars of High-CTR Thumbnail Psychology

1. **The 3-Second Visual Hook:**
   - A viewer scrolling at high speed must understand the emotional tension within 500 milliseconds on a mobile screen.
   - Never clutter: Maximum 1 primary subject + 1 secondary opposing subject.

2. **Visual Tension / Cognitive Dissonance:**
   - Create contrast that forces a question in the viewer's subconscious:
     - *Past vs. Present* (Caveman vs. Modern Human)
     - *Tension vs. Relief* (Looming giant predator vs. tiny unaware character)
     - *Expectation vs. Reality* (Normal routine vs. hidden evolutionary trap)

3. **Color Contrast & Luminance Pop:**
   - Dominant Background: Deep Indigo, Pitch Black, Obsidian, or High-Energy Golden Yellow (#F5C518).
   - Subject Rim-light: Electric Cyan (#00E5FF), Fiery Orange (#FF5722), or Gold Leaf.
   - Never use muddy or low-contrast blending.

4. **The 3-Word Text Rule:**
   - Text is optional, but if included: **Maximum 2–4 bold ALL CAPS words**.
   - The text must NOT repeat the video title — it must complement or reframe the title.
   - Placed in top-left or top-center (safe from YouTube's timestamp badge in bottom-right).
